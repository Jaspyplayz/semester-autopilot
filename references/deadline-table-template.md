# Deadline table template

Shown to the student in Step 2, before any plan is written, and saved as `deadlines.md` once confirmed. One row per dated assessment across all courses, soonest first. Keep it to these columns so it fits a phone screen; put extra detail in the course's plan.

Mark a value Claude inferred with "(assumed)" and a missing one with "TBA". Bold the next 7 days.

```markdown
# Deadlines, <Term>

Confirmed with you on <date>. "(assumed)" = not stated in the syllabus.

| When | Course | What | Worth | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fri Oct 16, 10:00** | **BIO 110** | **Quiz 2** | **5%** | **Units 3-4** |
| Wed Oct 21, 18:00 | HIS 201 | Midterm, room TBA | 25% | Weeks 1-6 (assumed) |
| Fri Oct 23, 23:59 | CS 150 | Assignment 2 | 10% | Loops, lists |

## Please check
- HIS 201 midterm: the syllabus doesn't say what it covers. I assumed Weeks 1-6. Right?
- HIS 201 midterm room is TBA. I'll leave it blank until it's posted.

Anything wrong, missing or that you're skipping?
```

Rules:
- One closing question only. Put item-specific questions in "Please check", one line each, at most 5. If there are more, ask about the next 3 weeks only.
- Undated items ("participation", "weekly readings") go in one line under the table, not as rows.
- When saving `deadlines.md` after the student answers, replace "Please check" and the closing question with a short "What you confirmed" list.
- Times use the school's time zone. Say it once in the header if the student might be elsewhere.
